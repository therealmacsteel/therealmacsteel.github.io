Why it matters

An assistant can only cite what it can read.

AI assistants answer from pages they can fetch and parse. A site that blocks their crawlers, hides its facts in images or never states them plainly gets left out of the answer.

What the fleet maintains

All of it is public.

The limits

What this can't promise

None of this guarantees a ranking, a citation or traffic. Assistants pick their own sources and change how they pick them. What it does is remove the reasons to leave a site out.

Questions

Does Mac Steel do SEO or AI-search work for other sites?

No. Mac Steel sells digital products and runs its own site. It does not take on client work.

Can I copy this setup?

Yes. Every file is public. Start with /llms.txt and /robots.txt, then view the source of any page for the structured data.

What is llms.txt?

A plain-text file at the root of a site that tells AI assistants what the site is and where its important pages are. It is a proposed convention, not a standard every assistant reads.

Is anything guaranteed?

No. Nothing here promises a ranking, a citation or traffic.
