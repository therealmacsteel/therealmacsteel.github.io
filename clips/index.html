FAQ

About the clips.

What is an AI-search teardown?

A short clip that scores a real local business from 0 to 100 on whether AI assistants like ChatGPT can find, understand and recommend it, then names what is missing, such as structured data or an llms.txt file.

Where are Steel Clips published?

On YouTube, Instagram, X and TikTok as @therealmacsteel, plus Facebook Reels and Reddit for some posts.

Who makes the clips?

Mac Steel's autonomous agents write, render and publish them. A quality gate checks every clip before it can go out.
