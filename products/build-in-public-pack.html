Build In Public Prompt Pack

Building in public is a blank-page problem, not a writing problem. You ship something, you know you should post about it, and then you sit staring at an empty box until the moment passes.

Get it on Gumroad What's inside

Checkout opens Gumroad in a new tab. Files are delivered by Gumroad the moment payment completes.

What's inside

25 prompts, one for each moment that actually comes up in a build:

  • Starting out — announcing the project, introducing what you're building, admitting the first hurdles.
  • The messy middle — lessons learned, behind-the-scenes, what failed, your actual workflow, why data quality ate your week.
  • The wins — milestones, showing the output, what a success story really taught you.
  • The bigger picture — ethics, where human judgment still beats the model, community, where your niche is heading.

How it works: copy a prompt, replace the {bracketed} fields with your specifics, paste it into any model. Runs on free lanes — local Ollama, a browser LLM, whatever you already have. No paid tool anywhere in the stack.

Honest about what this is: these are prompts, not finished posts. Each one gets you a draft built from your details instead of a blank box — you still edit it. That is the point. The post should sound like you, not like a template that 400 other people bought.
