Email Ops Prompt Pack: 10 Prompts for Planning, Checking and Fixing Email Sequences

Ten prompts for the operations side of email: mapping sequences to the events you really have, one job per email, checking a draft before it goes out, triaging replies and reading the results.

What's inside

Writing an email is the easy part; any AI assistant will give you a decent draft. What goes wrong is around the draft: a sequence that assumes events your store never sends, a welcome email that tries to do five jobs, a promise in a receipt that nothing in your system keeps, and replies that sit unread.

The 10 prompts, one job each:

  • 1 · Event-to-email map: which emails you need, from the events you really have
  • 2 · One-job welcome: a first email that does one thing
  • 3 · Checkout recovery: for people who start checkout and leave
  • 4 · Update announcement: tell buyers what changed, facts only
  • 5 · Win-back with a sunset rule: for the part of your list that stopped opening
  • 6 · Subject and preview set: lines you can actually test
  • 7 · Reply triage: sort the replies piling up in the inbox
  • 8 · Send preflight: a second look before a draft goes out
  • 9 · Plain-text and accessibility pass: the plain-text twin of an HTML email
  • 10 · Sequence review: read the numbers after a sequence has run

Every prompt has When, Expected output, Failure mode and Tip lines. The worked example chains two of the prompts on a free local model (gemma3:4b through Ollama), output shown unedited, including what it got wrong.

What this is not: an email platform or automation (nothing here sends mail), a promise of open rates, revenue or deliverability, or legal advice on email law. A person reads and sends every email; these prompts produce drafts and checks.
