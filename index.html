Straight answers

Before you ask

Who is SteelWorks for?

Any business whose revenue depends on being found, answering fast and running reliably online: local service firms, professional practices, agencies, e-commerce, software. The assessments are sized for a single business; builds and retainers scale from there.

Where does an engagement start?

With an assessment. The AI Search Readiness Audit is $97 and takes 72 hours. The AI Security & Risk Audit is $1,500 to $3,000. A broader Digital Intelligence Assessment is scoped through the intake form. Each produces a scored report you keep whether or not you go further.

Do I have to replace my website or my tools?

No. Every fix is written for the site and stack you already have. Operations builds run on hardware you own, with approval gates where a person should decide, and are handed back to you with documentation.

What we will not do

Invent reviews. Fake numbers. Promise what we cannot measure.

Is the security audit a compliance certification?

No. It scores your AI operation against the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and hands you owned, dated fixes. It is evidence for your own governance, not a certificate, and it does not guarantee that nothing can go wrong.

What will SteelWorks refuse to do?

Invent reviews, fake numbers, or claim a result that was not measured. If a check could not be run, the report says so and why. Retainers can be cancelled any month.

Who does the work?

SteelWorks Intelligence, Acworth GA. The audit engines, the agent fleet and the gates are ours; the judgement, the access and the accountability are a person's.
